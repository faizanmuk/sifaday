Put your personal birthday video in this folder as:

    assifa-birthday-video.mp4

and the poster image (the still shown before she presses play) in assets/photos/ as:

    assifa-birthday-video-poster.jpg

Paths can be changed in js/data.js -> video.
Tips: export as MP4 (H.264 + AAC), 1080p or 720p, ideally under ~60 MB.
A vertical phone video works too: set video.aspect to "9 / 16" (it also auto-detects).
Until the file exists, the site shows a placeholder instead.
